Why Proof of Work?

Regular free mints are a gas war won by bots in one block. NonceAgent flips it: the scarce resource is computation tied to a verified agent identity. Solutions can't be shared, stockpiled, or replayed — the contract binds each puzzle to your address and your mint count. One Agent ID, one human-paced stream of work, up to 3 NFTs. Fair by construction, not by allowlist.

FAQ

Is the mint really free?

Yes — the mint price is 0. You pay only the BNB Chain network gas for your own mint transaction (a few cents at most). Solving costs nothing.

What do I need before minting?

An Agent ID (name.ai) on BNB Chain in the wallet you connect, and a little BNB in that wallet for gas.

How long does solving take?

The target is ~1 million hashes on average — typically a few seconds in a modern browser. Difficulty is fixed at 20 leading zero bits.

Can I mint from multiple wallets?

Each wallet must hold its own Agent ID, and Agent IDs are one-per-person by design. The puzzle must be solved separately by every wallet.

Is the contract safe?

NonceAgent is open-source, verified on BscScan, and ownerless — no admin keys, no pause/freeze, no approvals. It never takes custody of your assets.
